# 🛍️ Customer Shopping Behavior Analysis

An end-to-end **Data Analytics project** that analyzes customer shopping behavior using **Python, MySQL, SQL, and Power BI**. The project focuses on understanding customer spending patterns, product preferences, customer segments, subscription behavior, and generating actionable business insights.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes transactional data containing **3,900 customer purchases** across different product categories.

The main objective is to transform raw customer shopping data into meaningful business insights that can support decision-making in areas such as:

- Customer segmentation
- Product performance
- Revenue analysis
- Subscription behavior
- Discount effectiveness
- Customer loyalty
- Age-group analysis
- Shipping preferences

## 🐍 1. Data Cleaning & Preparation

Python and Pandas were used for data preparation and exploratory analysis.

### Key Steps

- Loaded the dataset using Pandas
- Inspected the dataset using `df.info()` and `df.describe()`
- Checked for missing values
- Imputed missing Review Ratings using the median rating of each product category
- Standardized column names using `snake_case`
- Created an `age_group` feature
- Created `purchase_frequency_days`
- Checked data consistency
- Removed the redundant `promo_code_used` column
- Loaded the cleaned dataset into MySQL for further analysis

---

## 🗄️ 2. MySQL & SQL Analysis

The cleaned data was loaded into **MySQL** and SQL queries were used to answer important business questions.

### Business Questions

1. **Revenue by Gender**
   - Compared revenue generated by male and female customers.

2. **High-Spending Discount Users**
   - Identified customers who used discounts but still spent above the average purchase amount.

3. **Top 5 Products by Rating**
   - Identified products with the highest average review ratings.

4. **Shipping Type Comparison**
   - Compared average purchase amounts between Standard and Express shipping.

5. **Subscribers vs. Non-Subscribers**
   - Compared average spending and total revenue based on subscription status.

6. **Discount-Dependent Products**
   - Identified the products with the highest percentage of discounted purchases.

7. **Customer Segmentation**
   - Classified customers into:
     - New
     - Returning
     - Loyal

8. **Top 3 Products per Category**
   - Identified the most purchased products within each category.

9. **Repeat Buyers & Subscriptions**
   - Analyzed whether customers with more than five purchases were more likely to subscribe.

10. **Revenue by Age Group**
    - Calculated revenue contribution from different customer age groups.

## 💡 Key Insights

The analysis identified several useful business insights:

- Male customers generated higher total revenue than female customers in the analyzed dataset.
- Express shipping had a higher average purchase amount than Standard shipping.
- Customer segmentation showed a large proportion of customers in the Loyal segment.
- Young Adult customers contributed the highest revenue among the analyzed age groups.
- Certain products showed a higher dependency on discounts.
- Product ratings can help identify products suitable for promotional campaigns.
